Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal data, explaining here how it is collected and used.

1. Introduction:

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and personal data. This policy outlines how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our website or available services.

2. Information We Collect:

We collect certain information during registration or order placement, including:

  • Full Name.
  • Email Address.
  • Phone Number (if provided).
  • Target account details for service fulfillment.
  • Order history and payment records.

We do not collect any unnecessary data.

3. How We Use Your Data:

We utilize your data for the following purposes:

  • Fulfilling your orders accurately.
  • Communicating with you regarding order statuses.
  • Improving our overall service quality.
  • Providing responsive customer support.
  • Protecting against unauthorized usage or fraud.

We never use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Data Protection:

We employ administrative and technical security measures to safeguard your information, including:

  • End-to-end connection encryption (SSL).
  • Server firewall protection.
  • Preventing unauthorized system access.

However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, though we take all possible measures.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing:

We do not share your data with any external third parties except in the following cases:

  • Payment processors to execute valid financial transactions.
  • Compliance with legal obligations when officially required.
  • In cases of detecting fraudulent or malicious activities.

We strictly do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Cookies Policy:

Our site uses browser cookies to enhance your browsing experience, such as:

  • Saving your personal preferences.
  • Enabling automatic session login.
  • Tracking site performance analytics.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function properly.

7. User Rights:

You reserve the right to:

  • Access your stored personal data.
  • Modify or update your information.
  • Request full data deletion.
  • Permanently close your account.
  • Withdraw your consent for data collection.

To submit any request, please contact us via our Support page.

8. Account and Data Deletion:

  • You can request complete deletion of your data at any time, with responses handled within 7 days.
  • Once deleted, your account and associated data cannot be recovered.

9. Privacy Policy Updates:

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected in the "Last Updated" note at the bottom of the page.
